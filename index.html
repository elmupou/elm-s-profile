01 / Introduction

About Me

Mabuhay! Ang pangalan ko ay Lara, at ako ay nasa ikatlong taon na sa UPOU BAMS.

I enrolled in MMS 171 because I decided to slowly take production courses when I feel most ready. Since I am now in my junior year, I think this is the best time to learn production because I have already learned a lot from my previous major courses that I can apply to a production course.

I started with Text in Multimedia because text is one of the most common and foundational forms of media. I wanted to start with the basics. Text is important because although videos, music, and other media can easily get people's attention, text gives clarity to a message and helps inform people.

I am excited to learn how to place text better, combine it with other elements, and create harmony between them. I am looking forward to producing multimedia works where text stands out in a way that looks effective and professional.

03 / My Advocacy

Passion Projects

Environment & Multimedia

Environmental Sustainability Through Multimedia

I genuinely care about our environment, but I have been thinking about how I can encourage other people to care about it too. I want to show how environmental sustainability benefits not only nature but also us as humans. When we protect our natural resources, we also protect the resources that provide our needs and support our survival. If we continue to neglect and destroy our environment, we are also putting our own future at risk.

I know this may sound serious, but I believe it is a reality we need to understand. Some people may not immediately care about environmental protection, but showing how it directly affects their lives and benefits them might help them see its importance.

Through my multimedia skills, I want to create content that educates people around the world, especially those in remote communities. I want to explore how multimedia can be used not only to raise awareness but also to support environmental development, sustainability, and preservation.
